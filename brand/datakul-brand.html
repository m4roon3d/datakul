Brand sheet

Datakul

A gurukul for data careers, built on quest-based learning. The mark is the quest trail: a winding path of milestones climbing from where you start to the flag you're aiming for, on saffron. The wordmark is drawn as one rounded line and ends in a saffron dot, the next step.

Every topic a quest. Every quest a step closer.

Usage

A few rules

Do give it room

Keep clear space around the logo at least the height of the flag.

Do write it "Datakul"

Capital D in sentences. The wordmark itself is always lowercase, with its saffron dot.

Don't recolour the tile

The mark is always saffron with a white trail and an ink flag.

Don't use saffron for everything

In the app, saffron marks progress and wins. If everything glows, nothing does.
